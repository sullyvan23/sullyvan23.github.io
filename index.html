2026 Sumer X Shrine Bowl Analytics Competition Submission

Seperation Allowed Under Coverage Expectation (SAUCE)

Using provided tracking data from previous Shrine Bowl practices, I attempted to evaluate defensive back performance in the 1-on-1 drill vs wide receivers, and how much performance in the drill equates to NFL success. I created a model based off drill tracking data to project typical movement of a defensive back in response to wide receiver movement, distance, time since release, and more. I then compared actual results to projected average results from my model, giving defensive backs a score called Seperation Allowed Under Coverage Expectation (or SAUCE for short).

TCNJ Club Baseball Reference

As then Secretary and stat keeper of TCNJ Club Baseball (now co-President), I wanted to give my fellow teammates and alumni a fun way to view their own statistics. Using the National Club Baseball Association (NCBA) website, I compiled all recorded statistics in TCNJ Club Baseball history into a google sheet, and created sheets presenting stats in the format of sites such as Baseball Reference and Baseball Savant, along with estimates of statistics like OPS+, wOBA, and WAR.
